# Teaching AI (EdTech Books, 2026) — DDCT digest

**Source:** *Teaching AI: Syllabi, Pedagogy, and Best Practices in Higher Education* — peer-reviewed collection of **50 real course syllabi**, 4 sections (AI literacy · AI & writers · AI & educators · disciplinary practice). Full PDF (888 pp.) saved as `EdTech Books - Teaching AI Syllabi Collection 2026.pdf`. Digest based on deep reads of 6 DDCT-relevant chapters; chapter/page numbers refer to the PDF.

## The six picks for DDCT

### 1 · Ch35 "The Art of AI" (UCF, ART 3659C, online, 16 wks, p.601) — the AI-Fluency core for AI-skeptical creatives ★
- **GenAI tools withheld until week 7** — first half is critical literacy, ethics, history; "we grade the layers, versions, and ethical choices," process-over-product.
- **Week-1 "Temperature Check"**: unedited single-take video of raw feelings about "AI-generated art" (baseline re-evaluated week 13) — designed for exactly DDCT's polarized, social-media-informed students.
- **Anti-"broccoli framing"**: presenting AI as a mandatory evil puts students defensive; framed instead as a *situational tool* (nail-gun-vs-hammer analogy). "Curatorial mindset"; "AI Paradox" (technically original, conceptually derivative).
- **Bias taught safely**: "AI Scavenger Hunt" with FIXED vague prompts ("a portrait of a CEO") — stereotypes in output are undeniably the system's, not the student's.
- Integrity: AI permitted anywhere **if documented** (prompts, iterations, decisions). Final project: full agency — several students *choose* no AI = "creative sovereignty," celebrated.
- Ecosystem guardrails: Adobe/Firefly (content credentials, no training on student work), Glaze/Nightshade, C2PA, environmental footprint math.
- **DDCT:** S0 AI-Fluency core blueprint for the DD track (pairs with Lehigh's research-spine version); DR7 skeptic conversion; L3/L4.

### 2 · Ch36 "Audiovisual Storytelling with GenAI" (BAU Barcelona, 6×4h studio, 3rd/4th-yr design, p.618) — the AI-Augmented Production block ★
- One **narrative GenAI short film** (70% = film + written report + oral presentation) + 3 exercises (10% each): no-code story generator in Colab; **retraining a deliberately biased facial-recognition model** (LearningML); "AI StoryGaming" constraint-based collaborative creation.
- **Workflow mapping**: students draw their anticipated workflow at start and actual workflow at end — the judgment-notebook artifact as metacognition; the distance between maps is discussed.
- **Plural model ecology, paid ∥ open** (Krea/Runway/ComfyUI/Pinokio/HuggingFace) so exploration isn't conditioned on ability to pay. **Practitioner-confirmed failure:** students drifted to paid tiers; fix = institution-provided licenses + local ComfyUI/Pinokio infrastructure — independent validation of DDCT's local-lab strategy.
- Lesson learned: make **multi-model combination a structuring condition**, not an option (single-model films were weaker).
- Best practice: **screen 2–3 reference GenAI films every session, including previous students' work** — strongest observed motivator.
- Distributed-agency integrity: students "never asked to separate what the model did from what they did," only to document and make sense of it.
- **DDCT:** the Y3 AI-Augmented Production route in studio form; DR9/DR15 workflow design; L5.

### 3 · Ch46 "Gen AI in Research Methods" (SFSU, 14 wks, p.796) — the validation scaffold ★
- Every assignment = **pre-AI work → validation log → reflection**. Pre-AI stage exists because "grading that rewards finished product over visible process creates the incentive to outsource thinking" — the structural answer to offloading.
- **Ten named validation methods** (source triangulation, citation verification, self-consistency testing, cross-model consensus, atomic-claim verification, reasoning-consistency check, rule/constraint check, human-in-the-loop, temporal validity, pipeline citation) — a ready-made rubric for any DDCT AI-use log.
- The log is graded: "a student cannot earn full credit by accepting GenAI output uncritically."
- Institution-level ChatGPT Edu (CSU system deal) — the ASU/OpenAI model at system scale.
- **DDCT:** judgment-notebook operationalized; CT-track research methods; directly copyable into DDCT's 3-column protocol.

### 4 · Ch24 "Prompt Engineering for Writers" (KSU, grad, p.375) — prompting as rhetorical invention
- **"Rhetoric before tools"**: purpose/audience/genre/tone/context fixed *before* prompting; a prompt is "an act of rhetorical invention," not a command.
- Assessment = **portfolio of 6 documented use cases** (situation, initial prompt, output, revisions, editorial decisions, rationale) + public showcase (60/20/20).
- Policy: AI welcomed as "junior collaborator"; acknowledgment-not-citation; university Co-Pilot behind the firewall; Coursera micro-credentials embedded (institution pays).
- **DDCT:** DR4 — prompting taught as briefing/spec-writing (Direction Ladder: taste→critique→directing AI); the 6-case portfolio is a template for DDCT prompt-log grading.

### 5 · Ch1 "Talking Back to the Machine" (Hamline first-year seminar, p.24) — AI as first-year inquiry
- **Students write the course AI policy** (individual draft → class policy → semester-end reflection on their own use) — integrity built by authorship, not compliance; maps to DDCT's 3-zone policy being co-authored with students.
- Scaffolded team inquiry → campus research showcase; QQC (Quote-Question-Comment) pre-class reflections; day-1 "one word about GenAI" baseline ("tool" vs "cheating" clusters).
- **DDCT:** Y1 AI-Fluency framing where AI is the *subject of inquiry* that carries first-year skills (curiosity, sourcing, collaboration) — not tool training.

### 6 · Ch12 "The Aesthetics of AI" (U Chicago KNOW 36043, grad, p.181) — critical AI studies, research-as-practice
- Three modules **Knowledge / Aesthetics / Power**; Bauhaus→Media Lab lineage ("meet the technology on generative terms"); AI use **required** in every assignment with radical transparency.
- **AI Tool Analysis** reports accumulate into a public class website of "AI Co-Creation Guides" — the cohort builds a shared resource.
- Capstone dual path — creative artifact OR critical paper, both requiring a process-analysis report; "refusing to rank creative and scholarly work."
- **DDCT:** the DD+CT shared theory seminar (S4 research-creation); a model for how critique and making reinforce rather than compete.

## Cross-cutting patterns (convergent with the 83-paper library)
1. **Think-first structure everywhere** — tools delayed (ch35), rhetoric before tools (ch24), mandatory pre-AI work (ch46). Independently mirrors the brain-first/Maribor/unaided-bookends evidence.
2. **Documentation is the assessment** — validation logs, workflow maps, prompt portfolios: the judgment notebook appears in every strong syllabus, in a different guise each time.
3. **Policy co-authored with students** (ch1) beats imposed policy for buy-in.
4. **Equity via institution-provided access** — enterprise ChatGPT (ch46), firewall Co-Pilot (ch24), licenses + local models (ch36's fix). Confirms DDCT's local-lab + enterprise-tool moves.
5. **Bias taught through safe design** — fixed prompts (ch35) and biased-dataset retraining (ch36) instead of accusatory modules.
6. **Show exemplars, especially prior students' work** (ch36) — cheap, high-impact studio practice.
