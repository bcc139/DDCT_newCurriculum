Direction-First — the curriculum built on judgment

Visual companion to 03-direction-first-curriculum.md: if AI handles execution, the teachable center of a design degree moves to taste, critique, direction of humans + AI, product judgment, and the ability to fund yourself. Here is what that looks like as a ladder, a loop, a stack, and a timeline.

Solo devs = 21% of devs (GDC 2026) Indie = 40% of Steam releases / 35% of sales 🪜 6-rung direction ladder 🚀 incubator precedents: NYU · Drexel · RIT

1The skill shift — what a design degree is "about"

Evidence: AI investment concentrates in execution while "supervision and art direction show minimal AI investment" (Previs Office 2026) — the apex skill is now the core product of the degree.

2The Direction Ladder — six assessable rungs

Rungs 3–4 run in parallel on the same briefs: students direct AI agents and junior schoolmates side by side — one structure solves both "AI replaced juniors" and "juniors can't become seniors."

3AI-as-junior-staff — direction practice, industrialized

Graded metrics: acceptance rate, revision economy (iterations per accepted asset), notebook depth — never the raw AI output.

4The curriculum stack — theory spine + four practicum layers

The Atelier (Scenario 4) is the natural host; Track A of Scenario 1 renames to "Craft & Direction" on this stack.
