## Top 3 cases for the DDCT curriculum

1. **Lehigh BSTA 495/395 AI-assisted-research course** — 13 weeks, 4 cumulative modules over an authentic workflow, AI-use log + attribution template on every submission, 4-dimension rubric with authorial-mastery viva, ethics-as-subject, experience-before-taxonomy sequencing. Measured: attribution d=+2.40, hallucination-detection d=+1.45, 88% transfer intent (self-report, no control). Strengthens **S0 AI-Fluency core + S5-L1** (DR3, DR23): clone the architecture, swap the spine to a DD/CT production pipeline.
2. **AIAS 5-level scale with structural implementation** — per-brief levels with mechanics changes (prompt rationales, storyboard artefacts, staged checkpoints, institution-provided tools); storyboard = 25% of grade at Level 2. Deployed at 300+ institutions, 30 languages, TEQSA-recognized, pilot evidence of reduced misconduct. Strengthens **S0 3-zone policy** (DR23/DR28/DR12): subdivide the yellow zone into Levels 2–4, make Level 5 the S4 capstone mode, use institutional tool provision as the local-model-lab equity argument.
3. **Klapproth 3-column AI-use protocol + close-without-AI rule** — document (own start | AI contribution | justified adopt/modify/reject), graded on decision quality; every AI-supported sequence ends with an unaided parallel task; 4-option AI rule derived from the intended learning outcome. Backed by: retention drop 57.5% vs 68.5% (d=0.68) under unrestricted ChatGPT; failure 2–6%→18.4% when supervision added. Strengthens **DR23/L2/L3/L6**: this is the judgment notebook of the AI-as-junior-staff loop, operationalized, plus the cheapest fundamental-protecting rule DDCT can adopt studio-wide.

*Gaps: no 3D/creative-production assessments in the folder (storyboard + dance vignettes are the only transferable art cases); skills/ethics effects consistently weakest (g≈0.64–0.67); detector consensus unanimously anti-surveillance; two framework papers report zero numbers; Lehigh effects are self-reported single-cohort.*
