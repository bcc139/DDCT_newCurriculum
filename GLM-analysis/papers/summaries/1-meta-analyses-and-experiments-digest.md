## Top 3 cases for the DDCT curriculum

1. **"Maribor package" — assessment redesign that safely absorbs ChatGPT** (Kosar 2024; corroborated in Hawi & Samaha 2026). AI-resistant briefs (UML/given code) + AI-free in-lab extensions + oral defense + paper exams → n=182, all outcomes null (p≥0.730), i.e., permission made harmless and usage self-regulated. Strengthens **S0 3-zone + DR23 + L5**: the 🔴 defense zone is what licenses a permissive 🟢 zone.
2. **Attempt-first, hints-not-answers AI tutor wrappers** (Lee 2024 / Makransky 2025 / Kestin 2025 via Hawi & Samaha; G–P–T variant from Yang 2025). Initial attempt required, then guided hints/teach-back/bi-directional annotation → better SRL + HOT than generic ChatGPT; physics tutor beat in-class active learning in *less* time; raw access control lost achievement/flow/self-efficacy. Strengthens **L1/L3 + S1 AI-Augmented Production + S5 L2 blocks** — the studio form of the AI-as-junior-staff brief→draft→critique→gate loop.
3. **Counterbalanced AI-on/AI-off lab blocks with a published multidimensional rubric** (Andleeb 2025). Alternate ChatGPT/no-ChatGPT across consecutive assignments, scored on correctness/efficiency/readability/warnings/errors (κ=0.87) + timed work + concept checks → quality up (p≤0.018), time cut 2.2–4.8×, concepts flat-to-mixed. Strengthens **S0 3-week sprints + DR23/DR9** — the evaluation pattern lets students *measure* their own AI delta; rubric doubles as QC-grade habit.

## Gaps / notes
- Meta spread SMD 0.45 → g 0.804 (→0.321 bias-corrected); duration findings conflict (8–16wk peak vs short>long) — read as "context dominates."
- Metacognition contradiction (n.s. in Chen & Cheung vs 0.74 in Han) → never assume AI builds metacognition without explicit reflection scaffolds.
- Programming nulls cluster in large/long studies (Kosar, Jošt, Gambo R²=0.006); positives cluster in small/short tasks on speed & quality, not concepts.
- Creativity weakest HOT gain (g=0.444) and visual tools weakest content type (0.510) — no experimental evidence yet for generative image/video/3D in art-school pipelines.
- Traffic-light policy + disclosure table = interview-level evidence (mirrors DDCT 3-zone), no outcome numbers yet. Venues for papers 7/9/10 are not printed in the converted texts.
