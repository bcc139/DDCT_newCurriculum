# 8-writing-and-composition — digest (15 papers, 2026-10-13)

**2024 Fostering AI Literacies in the Composition Classroom** (KSU masters project; surveys n=1,126 + 33-pilot + teacher journal) | Structured AI-integrated semester: improved perception 67.86%, negative sentiment 9.09%→3.03%, 85.71% open to continued use; but 45.16% less likely to seek instructor help | S0/DR23/DR7/L4 ★ — copy the day-one "AI is Your Friend (sometimes)" policy memo + Five-Canons AI assignments; structured exposure converts skeptics.
**2025 AI Tools in Society — Cognitive Offloading and Critical Thinking** (Gerlich, Societies 15:6; n=666 UK + 50 interviews) | AI use↔critical thinking r=−0.68; offloading↔CT r=−0.75; indirect effect b=−0.25 of total −0.42; younger = more use, lower CT; sample 48% doctorate | DR3/L6/Brake·Anchor·Engine — canonical citation for 🔴 zones and unaided-practice requirements (correlational only).
**2025 Generative AI-Assisted Feedback and EFL Writing — Grammarly RCT** (Mekheimer, Discover Educ. 4:170; n=60 Egypt) | Post-test 54.37 vs 45.50/60 (t=10.89, p<.001, MD 8.87); Language Use MD 5.45, Organization MD 2.47, Content ns; AI-use↔revisions r=0.42 | L1/L2 ★ — 🟡-zone feedback tool for Thai EFL writers with 60-min training + "critique-then-apply" rule; keep higher-order feedback human.
**2025 University students describe how they adopt AI** (Black & Tomlinson, IJAIED; 39/277, UC Irvine) | Revising/editing 47 instances vs ghostwriting 24, skepticism 12; students invented "Is the grammar correct?" prompting; AI citations often "wrong or did not exist" | DR23/L5 ★ — require the "Use of AI" disclosure section (system, how, prompts, self-assessed impact) in every 🟡 assignment.
**2025 Your Brain on ChatGPT — Cognitive Debt** (Kosmyna et al., MIT, arXiv preprint; EEG, n=54, 4 sessions, crossover n=18) | 83.3% of LLM-first writers couldn't quote their own essay vs 11.1% unaided (p<.001); ownership 9/18 full vs 16–18/18; connectivity Brain>Search>LLM; brain-first→AI-later = network-wide spike + high recall | DR3/L6/🔴 zone ★ — sequence every AI task brain-first-then-AI; cite with preprint caveat.
**2026 AI-Mediated Feedback Improves Student Revisions — FeedbackWriter RCT** (Lu et al., CHI'26; N=354, 11 TAs, 1,366 essays) | Revision quality d=0.50 (50th→70th percentile); post-test ns; TAs adopted 51.3% of AI comments, approved 88.7% of 24,270 judgments; raw AI feedback gets >40% student rejection vs <20% for instructor | L1/L2/DR14 ★ — AI-as-junior-staff quality gate: rubric-anchored suggestions, empty-by-default boxes, TA adopt/edit/dismiss; studio critique + capstone feedback at scale.
**2026 Cognitive Offloading Through Digital Tools** (Wang, Front. Psychol. 17:1781101; SEM, n=468 Chinese undergrads) | Offloading→self-efficacy β=0.31; self-efficacy→CT β=0.41; direct offloading effects ns after mediators — contradicts Gerlich | DR3/L6 nuance — harm is offloading-without-self-efficacy; frame AI as competence-builder, not answer machine.
**2026 Designing AI-Resilient Assessment — Four-Pillar Framework** (Nikolić & Basta Nikolić, Front. AI 9:1841682; conceptual) | Rubric: reasoning 25–35% / oral defense 25–35% / process 20–30% / product ≤20%; viva protocol 8–12 min (17–35 h per 100 students; 30–40% sampling); Prohibited/Guided/Permitted taxonomy; 6-question vulnerability audit | DR23/3-zone/tickets-vivas ★ — most ready-to-adopt kit for S0/S4 capstones.
**2026 Evaluating Accuracy and Reliability of AI Content Detectors** (Hadra et al., Sultan Qaboos; 192 texts, Turnitin vs Originality) | Overall acc 0.69 vs 0.61; hybrid recall 0.02 (Originality) / 0.31 (Turnitin); science-genre acc drops to 0.51–0.58 vs 0.86–0.96 humanities; Originality EFL bias borderline (p=0.058) | DR23/L5 — detectors = triage never verdict; science/technical writing (DDCT's domain) is worst-detected.
**2026 Examining AI-assisted writing revision through assignment design** (Heather et al., Front. Educ. 11:1879758; 49/111, UC Davis) | Two-step assignment: unaided draft = 80% grade (human feedback), AI revision = 10%, reflection = 10%; students evolved multi-tool "toolbox" + targeted voice-preserving prompts | S0 3-zone sequencing (🔴→🟡)/DR23/L3 ★ — grade-weighting is the lever: price unaided craft above AI polish.
**2026 Generative AI Offers More but Students Revise Less** (Farrokhnia et al.; randomized, n=70, Persian, teacher vs zero-shot vs CoT GPT-4o) | CoT feedback quality > teacher, but revision gains ns by condition (p=.30, ηp²=.03); teacher feedback quality predicted revision (B=0.57, p=.003), AI's didn't; AI feedback tracked initial draft quality (r=0.53) → Matthew effect | L1/DR14 ★ — use the appendix CoT+rubric+exemplar prompt template for AI critique; caution: weaker students get weaker AI feedback; non-English degradation.
**2026 Reimagining Writing Assessment for the AI Era — Systematic Review** (Abdellatif et al., Front. Psychol. 17:1809174; PRISMA, 1,756→19 studies) | ChatGPT 88.8% / Grammarly 67.4% adoption; institutional guidance 67% faculty vs 17.8% student-facing; misuse driven by policy voids + time pressure, not malice; redesign menu: vivas, process-product, multimodal, PBL | DR23/DR26/L5 — justifies student-facing AI-Fluency core course (S0).
**2026 Revising with AI — Aligning Feedback Technologies with Revision Processes** (Helm & Hesse, Front. Educ. 11:1702406; conceptual, Writing Coach + FelloFish) | 48–62% ignore AI feedback (FelloFish: 6,889/14,236 = 48% zero changes at "≥1 character" threshold); surface uptake 86% vs meaning-level 32%; 3 tensions (timing vs critical distance, agency, communicative embedding) | L3/DR14 — delay AI critique until students critique exemplars and self-diagnose first.
**2026 Stop Perfecting the Feedback Start Supporting the Uptake** (Helm & Hesse, Front. Educ. 11:1737037; position paper) | 5-phase model: draft → AI feedback → individual revision → peer discussion → plenary reflection on AI comments → final revision; teacher as facilitator who can publicly disagree with the AI | S4 studio critique + AI-as-junior-staff loop (L3/L8) ★ — drop-in format for vertical studios/3-week blocks; absorbs AI-hostile students by making AI critique discussable.
**2026 Who Wrote This — Reliability of AI Detection Tools** (Van Vlasselaer et al., VUB; preregistered, 160 docs × 4 detectors + 1,163 real theses) | Pangram: full-AI 65%/97.5%, hybrid 92.5%; Turnitin 0% on Deep-Research texts; GPTZero ~0–2.5%; zero false positives (3/4 tools); real corpus: 45.5% theses flagged, mean 34% AI, >80% in only 1.9% | DR23/L5 ★ — hybrid authorship is the norm; pair any detection scan with vivas + process logs; benchmark-and-scan method is itself transferable institutional practice.

## Top 3 cases for the DDCT curriculum
1. **FeedbackWriter TA-in-the-loop feedback (CHI'26 RCT)** — rubric-anchored GPT-4o suggestions, empty-by-default boxes, TA adopt/edit/dismiss; d=0.50 revision quality, 88.7% judgment agreement, 51.3% adoption. Strengthens S0/S1 studio critique + AI-as-junior-staff quality gate (L1/L2, DR14): AI drafts critique against the brief's rubric, instructor runs the gate, students never see raw AI output.
2. **Brain-first sequencing, grade-weighted 80/10/10** — unaided draft (80%, human feedback) → AI revision (10%) → reflection (10%) (UC Davis); MIT EEG crossover confirms unaided-first→AI-later is the neurocognitively optimal order (83.3% vs 11.1% quote failure; connectivity spike). Strengthens DR23 + 3-zone sequencing (🔴→🟡) + L6: make this the default assignment skeleton in both DD and CT.
3. **Four-pillar AI-resilient assessment kit** — weighted rubric (product ≤20%), 8–12 min viva protocol with workload math and 30–40% random sampling, Prohibited/Guided/Permitted AI taxonomy with disclosure, 6-question vulnerability audit. Strengthens DR23 + 3-zone policy + tickets/vivas in S0/S4 (design instruments, not yet validated — pilot it).

**Gaps:** Gerlich vs Wang offloading contradiction (population/measure dependent); detector rankings disagree across studies (model/corpus snapshot — hybrid texts hardest everywhere except Pangram); MIT study is a non-peer-reviewed preprint (n=18/group); no study shows durable learning gains from student-facing AI feedback (uptake 48–62% failure, meaning-level 32%); all text-domain — transfer to 3D/animation is structural, not content; several small samples and researcher-as-rater confounds (Mekheimer n=60, Farrokhnia n=70 with abstract/method inconsistency); nothing on visual/3D detection, IP fog, or NDA-safe local models (DR9–16 gaps).
