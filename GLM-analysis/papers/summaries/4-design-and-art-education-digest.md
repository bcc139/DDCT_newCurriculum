## Top 3 cases for the DDCT curriculum

1. **DDDOS per-stage AI-gating sheet (Song & Xu 2026)** — a Double-Diamond operation sheet telling students which AI tool is allowed at which stage + self-monitoring questions + completion checks, no extra instructor time → achievement 20.90 vs 18.65 (GAI-only) vs 16.13 (traditional), p<.001; strengthens S0 AI-Fluency core/3-week sprints, DR15/DR23 — it operationalizes the 3-zone policy inside a single studio brief (image-gen banned during Define to prevent premature fixation).
2. **Three-integration-mode quasi-experiment (Li & Chen 2026)** — traditional vs ideation-only chatbots vs full-workflow AIGC in a real book-design course (n=100) → full integration wins products (85.34 vs ~75–76) and role clarity, but the chatbot arm is no better than traditional on products and significantly worse on self-efficacy/role ambiguity; strengthens L2 and the 3-zone policy (S0/S1): for AI-skeptical students, either fully scaffolded role-explicit workflows or AI-free — never sprinkle chatbots.
3. **Student-trained LoRA customization lab (Li, Sun & Kim 2025)** — 96 students each trained a personal LoRA (20 images @512px, ~40 min, repeat=10/epoch=10/lr=1e-4, one RTX 4060 Ti) inside a 6-week Double-Diamond poster course with sketch-before-AI gate and logged prompt rationales → engagement up on all three dimensions (cognitive Δ+0.472, p<.001), anxiety locus shifted from outputs to configuration, career confidence grew; strengthens S0 local-model lab (DR11 NDA-safe), DR22 Thai-culture LoRAs as differentiating assets, DR27.
