0INFUSE — retrofit DD & CT in place

Keep both programs; add five layers around them. The fastest, lowest-risk move — and the foundation every other scenario reuses.

⏱ 12 months💰 low cost👥 low-mid faculty load⚠️ low risk🎓 all current students
Five additive layers — the degree spine itself is untouched, so approvals stay at course level.

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • AI Fluency course design (4 principles: delegation, description, discernment, diligence) — Ringling's certificate co-developed with Anthropic + University College Cork: CGW article
  • UNESCO AI competency framework for students (map course outcomes to it): unesco.org · for teachers: framework for teachers
  • AI-tool use policy, published — Futuregames' responsible-use policy page: futuregames.se
  • Portfolio policy language ("AI generated art is not allowed") — DigiPen BFA admissions: digipen.edu · Sheridan's equivalent naming Midjourney/ChatGPT: sheridancollege.ca
  • Local-model lab precedent (NDA-safe, sovereignty) — FABW's non-cloud AI platform + ethics framework: filmakademie.de
  • "Mechanisms over menu" course design — DigiPen CS 394/594 How Generative AI Works: digipen.edu
  • First-month AI restriction (foundations integrity) — Digital Hollywood University via Mainichi: mainichi.jp

🧭 First three moves

  1. Draft the 3-zone policy + stand up the AI Curriculum Council with student seats (one semester).
  2. Write the AI-Fluency syllabus against UNESCO's framework; pilot it as a 3-week block before making it a required course.
  3. Buy/provision one local-model GPU node; install open-weight image/video/LLM stack; write the NDA-safe usage guide.

Design requirements met: DR3 (taste before tools) · DR8 (legitimate AI-free space) · DR9 (honest pipeline map) · DR11–DR13, DR16 (ethics, local models, evaluation, disclosure) · DR23 (3-zone integrity) · DR24–DR26 (faculty ladder, tool budgeting, fast blocks). Not met: DR1/DR4 (apprenticeship), DR22 (differentiation).

1TWO-TRACK UNDERGRAD — fork at Year 3, merge at Year 4

Your fine-arts/digital-arts analogy, corrected by the global evidence: split students late (after shared foundations) and by destination, never at intake — then force the tracks to need each other in a mixed capstone.

⏱ 18–24 months💰 medium cost👥 medium faculty load⚠️ medium risk🎓 Y3+ students
Track names sell destinations, not tools — the lesson from DigiPen (program-level split) and SCAD's controversy (avoid ideology branding).

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • Shared-then-fork precedent + mixed teams: DigiPen embeds yearly cross-disciplinary game teams (art + design + CS + sound) — see BS-in-AI program: digipen.edu and its CS-and-Game-Design sequence showing AI-for-Games placement: sample sequence
  • What an intake-level split costs you politically: Fast Company on SCAD's Applied-AI-major controversy: fastcompany.com · the gentler minor route: Georgia Entertainment
  • Critical-lens courses for Track A (AI as object of study): RPI Critical Game Design: hass.rpi.edu · Tampere's four perspectives: tuni.fi
  • Control-technique curriculum for Track B: DigiPen CS 394/594: digipen.edu · practitioner pipeline guide (ComfyUI/ControlNet/USD): GenAI VFX whitepaper
  • Uppsala's organizational note: even production game design can live in a humanities faculty — useful for naming the tracks: uu.se

🧭 First three moves

  1. Write the two Y3 track maps as concentration sheets inside DD/CT (avoids new-degree approvals).
  2. Pick one Y4 capstone brief that structurally requires both tracks (e.g., AI-augmented Thai folklore short) and staff it with one Track-A lead + one Track-B lead as co-advisors.
  3. Run an information campaign *before* Y2 students choose — present tracks as destinations ("art director" vs "technical director"), never as pro/anti AI.

Design requirements met: DR1/DR4 partially (capstone hierarchy) · DR3 (AI-off core) · DR5–DR6 (role clarity, judgment work) · DR8 (no intake split) · DR9–DR10, DR13–DR14 (honest AI teaching) · DR22 (named destinations). Needs: Scenario 4's vertical studios for the full ladder.

2NEW B.Sc. — Creative Technology & AI

A four-year sibling to CT, using the DigiPen BS-in-AI blueprint your partnership gives you direct access to — with a Thai-content twist for differentiation.

⏱ 24–36 months (incl. approvals)💰 high cost👥 high faculty load⚠️ high risk🎓 new intake
Blueprint: DigiPen's BS in CS in AI (course ladder: ML I → Deep Learning incl. LLMs/generative models → concentrations), adapted with a production spine.

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • The blueprint itself — DigiPen BS in Computer Science in AI (course codes CS 372 / CS 376 / MAT 345; concentrations incl. Neural Networks-LLMs): digipen.edu — requestable in full via your DigiPen channel
  • Graduate-level analogue with GenAI focus — NTU ADM MSc Game Design, Art & Technology: ntu.edu.sg
  • What an AI-major launch looks like politically — SCAD Applied AI (BDes): program · the controversy
  • Deep-technical course model — DigiPen online MS in AI (30-credit structure): online.digipen.edu · CS 394/594 (engine embedding, hallucination reduction): course article
  • UK MSc showing the ML-for-media unit mix (ML for Media Production, Generative AI for Media): Bournemouth NCCA

🧭 First three moves

  1. Formally request the DigiPen BS-in-AI degree map + CS 394/594 syllabus through the DDCT–DigiPen channel (see 02-curriculum-scenarios.md §9).
  2. Pilot the Y3 "Generative Media" semester as Scenario-0 tool-sprint blocks; use enrollment as the go/no-go signal.
  3. Recruit 1–2 ML-capable faculty or visiting industry TDs before approvals — the degree is only as real as its Y2–3 staffing.

Design requirements met: DR5–DR7 (new-role skills, tool-learning speed) · DR9, DR13–DR14 (mechanisms, evaluation, durable layers) · DR17/DR19–DR22 (value-chain lift, compliance, differentiation). Risks: DR8 (avoid branding as "the AI degree" for hostile students — position as CT's elite sibling).

3GRADUATE — M.Sc./M.F.A. Creative AI + AnT-style certificate

The highest-urgency piece: Thailand's digital-content headcount is already shrinking due to AI while the industry grows — the incumbents need reskilling now, and a graduate layer builds your research capacity.

⏱ 18–30 months💰 medium cost (industry-co-fundable)👥 medium load⚠️ medium risk🎓 professionals + researchers
Urgency math: Thai digital content = 50.6 B฿ and growing ~10%/yr, while headcount fell 1% (7,688) "due to the adoption of AI" — someone must retrain the incumbents; better you than nobody.

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • The AnT template — DigiPen Singapore's Attach-and-Train launch (retrains STEM professionals into AI software developers for games; Specialist Diploma; partner companies): launch · graduate outcomes (4 mo class + 2 mo OJT)
  • Short professional course shape — NFTS "AI for Film and TV: Tools, Protocols and Practices" (6-month part-time online): nfts.co.uk
  • Industry-brief learning — CDM's Inworld AI master's project (students solve live AI-NPC briefs): thecdm.ca
  • Creative-component thesis architecture — NTU ADM MA (Research): ntu.edu.sg · Concordia's Research-Creation PhD stream (studio exam + artwork + 150–200pp text): concordia.ca
  • Thailand urgency data — depa via Nation (50.6 B฿, −1% workforce due to AI): nationthailand.com · TACGA animation contraction: Bangkok Post

🧭 First three moves

  1. Hold a joint workshop with depa + 3–5 TACGA studios to size the AnT cohort (who would sponsor seats?).
  2. Ask DigiPen (Singapore campus) for the AnT partner-company playbook via your channel.
  3. Approve the M.Sc. skeleton with the two exit routes; start with the practice route only (research route needs supervisor capacity you build meanwhile).

Design requirements met: DR18 (serve the incumbent workforce) · DR1/DR4 (adults apprentice on real briefs) · DR5, DR11, DR19 (new roles, compliance, client-ready) · DR22 (first-mover differentiation in Thailand). Note: undergrad resistance is irrelevant here — adults opt in.

4MISSION-DRIVEN ATELIER — the unorthodox pilot

Your instinct, assembled from four proven mechanisms: Gallatin's matched advisers, Aalto Design Factory's passion-based PBL, the Colorado Block Plan's 3-week intensives, and DigiPen-style vertical teams — with competency badges as the transcript.

⏱ pilot in 12 months💰 medium cost👥 high advising load⚠️ medium risk (registrar friction)🎓 volunteer cohort 20–30
Four imported mechanisms, one engine. Detail: 02-curriculum-scenarios.md §Scenario 4 & §7.

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • Matched-adviser individualized study — NYU Gallatin ("how Gallatin works": student-authored concentration + primary faculty adviser): gallatin.nyu.edu · plain-language overview: meet.nyu.edu
  • Passion/problem-based atelier platform — Aalto Design Factory (PBL, industry co-creation, bootcamps): designfactory.aalto.fi
  • Block teaching — THE's practitioner guide: guide · 50-year Colorado College Block Plan retrospective: collection · honest critique (retention tail-off → add spaced re-touch): critique · design guidance: Queen Mary Academy
  • Vertical teams — DigiPen's yearly cross-disciplinary game projects: digipen.edu
  • Craft-first missions with full academic legitimacy — RPI Critical Game Design (making + critique in one degree): hass.rpi.edu · the critical-gameplay art lineage: criticalgameplay.com

🧭 First three moves

  1. Recruit the pilot honestly: call for 20–30 volunteers across Y2–Y4; explicitly invite craft-first missions.
  2. Write the badge taxonomy (5 competencies × 3 levels) with assessment rubrics — this is the piece registrars will ask about.
  3. Negotiate one registrar concession: block-scheduled terms + badge transcript for the pilot cohort only, evaluated after year 1.

Design requirements met: DR1/DR4 fully (the ladder lives here) · DR7 (blocks = learn-a-tool-fast) · DR8 (authorship converts resistance) · DR2 (badges assess process/judgment) · DR22 (distinctive identity). Main cost: advising hours — cap student-adviser ratios at ~8:1.

5THAI CREATIVE-AI ACADEMY — the alliance ladder

KMUTT-hosted, industry-co-funded micro-credentials open to every KMUTT student, partner universities, and working professionals — Taiwan's TAICA proves the model at national scale.

⏱ 9–12 months to L1–L2💰 low-medium (sponsorable)👥 medium load⚠️ low risk🎓 everyone + industry
Start L1 within a year — it doubles as Scenario 0's fluency course and the alliance's front door.

📚 Precedents & further reading

  • The national-scale proof — Taiwan AI College Alliance (cross-university AI credit programs, certificates): TAICA at Yuan Ze · lead-course listing: NTHU AIA courses
  • Continuing-education micro-credential shape — DigiPen Singapore's 3-month deep-learning micro-credential: digipen.edu.sg
  • Sponsored industry briefs as a course format — CDM × Inworld AI: thecdm.ca
  • Short-course depth exemplar — ArtFX's AI intro inside a professional master's: artfx.school · Utrecht's game-AI summer course: utrechtsummerschool.nl
  • Fluency-course design — Ringling/Anthropic AI Fluency: cgw.com · UNESCO student framework: unesco.org

🧭 First three moves

  1. Run L1 as a paid summer bootcamp for 60 mixed participants (KMUTT + 2 partner unis + TACGA members) — cash-flow positive from day one.
  2. Sign the first cross-crediting MOU with 2–3 partner universities (copy TAICA's program-certificate structure).
  3. Sell one L3 sponsored brief to a studio (their IP, your supervision, students credited) — the demo that recruits the next sponsors.

Design requirements met: DR18 (incumbent reskilling at scale) · DR7 (blocks) · DR24–DR26 (fast layer, partnership funding) · DR8 (L1 as non-ideological entry). Watch: credit-recognition negotiations are the long pole — start them in month 1.
